# External design review #1: Gemini 4 Neon (2026-11-26): triage

The full review is in [gemini4neon_review_2026-11-26.md](gemini4neon_review_2026-11-26.md). It was produced by `rnd/external_review.py`: a 207 kB
packet (scorecard, decisions, 20 reports, params.py, all part CAD) plus 2 renders, sent to `gemini-4-neon`, reasoning high.

**Verdict:** the review is high value. It found a class of problem our checks don't test, **assembly sequence and serviceability**, plus several
safety-electronics gaps. Each finding is triaged below: **V** = verified in the repo, **P** = plausible but not yet checked, **D** = disputed (with reason).

| # | Finding (severity) | Triage | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trapped bearings / monolithic shafts: bearings can't be installed; no keyed torque transfer shaft→link (critical) | **V** (small_hub: 61804 journals sit between a Ø87 hub and Ø36 pulley flanges) | Queue A1: split hubs/pulleys and bolt them; keys/D-flats; add an **assembly-sequence check** to rnd/integration.py |
| 2 | KX print-in-place: the spring pocket is too small, the balls aren't captured, the pause order is wrong (critical) | P (needs a geometric check) | Queue A2: verify; likely move to a screwed retainer + a real spring pocket (6–8 mm) |
| 3 | KX creep / tooth stress at 55–70 °C under-estimated (major) | P: consistent with loop 29 (thin peak margin) | C1 creep hold at 65 °C / 168 h; thicker teeth; PETG-CF/PA-CF for KX parts |
| 4 | Clock-spring cavities don't exist in CAD; yaw free length ~145 mm not 240; a long CSI FFC is risky; mast/base moving harness missing (critical) | V (cavities are analysis-only; the FFC length point is fair) | Queue A3: model the cavities; re-measure the yaw run; switch to USB2 UVC on a slim cable (reverse the D12 FFC part); mast drag chain |
| 5 | Wrist pitch/roll and head tilt carry bending on actuator shafts (no 61805s in CAD); head tilt 75 % of servo rating (major) | **V** (61805 only in the mass list) | Queue A4: add the wrist bearing pairs; move the head tilt axis under the head COM + passive pivot |
| 6 | E-stop: 0.5 s hold-up, no regen clamp, no pre-charge, 12 V servos bypass the contactor (critical) | V (as designed: SS1 with 0.5 s; no clamp / pre-charge in the plan) | Queue A5: regen clamp + brake resistor, pre-charge, servos downstream, STO review |
| 7 | Thermal model ignores PETG cans + enclosed shells; PETG HDT ~65–70 °C (critical) | P: fair. Our 100 °C was the winding limit, not the PETG limit | Queue A6: PETG-side temperature limit 55 °C, vented cans, aluminium motor plate, bench test |
| 8 | Roll stage 20T in CAD vs 18T in belts/BOM; straight-sided printed teeth; small_hub interlayer shear never FEA'd (critical) | **V** (roll.py uses small_hub() default 20T) | Queue A7: fix teeth; consider aluminium HTD small pulleys on steel stubs; FEA small_hub |
| 9 | FEA: 99.5th-percentile truncation + P1 tets on thin walls hide peaks / under-predict deflection; hand sag likely 8–20 mm (major) | P: fair on method (we report max too) | Queue A8: P2 tets; peak stress at fillets; full-chain compliance (was queue item 3) |
| 10 | Balancer spring can't fit the 135 mm cage; Ø16 ungrooved pulley; arm-detach snap hazard (major) | **V** (balancer.py assumes 105 mm stroke in a 135 mm cage) | Queue A9: re-design the balancer (vertical spring/extension springs/gas strut), grooved pulley, park-lock pin |
| 11 | Tip margin untested for 2.5 kg bimanual + 45° azimuth + braking + human nudge (major) | P | Queue A10: add those cases to twin/mc |
| 12 | Threshold MC saturates the wheel motors (1.00×); KX on wheels could slip; skirt clearance on carpet; deck quarters not tied (major) | V (1.00× is in threshold_mc.md) | Queue A11: drop KX on the wheels (bolt hubs), steel/Al chassis ring, raise skirt clearance |
| 13 | Dock: the bumper_light ring clashes; the frictionless-slide sim; floor screws (major) | **V** (ring R 250–254 at z 43–53, between the pads) | Queue A12: cut the ring at the pads; ramp-baseplate dock; re-sim with wheel friction |
| 14 | BMS undersized / no telemetry; 4.15 V stop prevents balancing; blind-mate XT30 risks (major) | P | Queue A13: smart BMS with telemetry; periodic 4.20 V balance charge; umbilical plug |
| 15 | CAN load ~76 % at 500 Hz (major) | V (numbers from electrical.md) | Queue A14: 250 Hz on base/lift/sensors; bench latency test |
| 16 | Lift ratchet: bonded PETG teeth on Al; drop/pinch hazards; open belt nips (critical) | P | Queue A15: bolted steel rack + metal pawl, or a fail-safe EM brake; belt guards |
| 17 | Printability: bed-edge warping, support-heavy brackets; build time realistically 45–70 h (major) | P | Fold into A1 (flat bolt-together plates) and R10 |

**Effect on confidence:** the review shows the CAD is **not yet buildable as drawn** (finding 1). Several sub-systems need a second design pass
(KX socket construction, balancer, lift lock, e-stop electronics). Until queue A1–A6 close, "works on the first full build" is lower than stated
in confidence.md. The staged path (coupons → M1 bench arm) is unchanged, and the reviewer rated it a strength.
